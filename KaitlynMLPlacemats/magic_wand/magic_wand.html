Attach a Double Motor to your wand, teach it a few swishes, then see how it guesses who's swishing — and what happens when someone practices a lot more than someone else.

This browser can't connect to the wand

Web Bluetooth is needed to talk to the LEGO hub, and it's only available in Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone (not Safari, not iPhone/iPad, not Firefox). Try opening this page in Chrome.

1. Connect your wand

Power on the Double Motor attached to your wand, then connect. Its button light will turn gold once it's connected so you know which hub is which.

If several wands are powered on nearby, matching the color and number printed on your Connection Card makes sure you connect to the right one. Leave blank to just pick from a list.

2. Record swishes

Type a name, hit record, then swish the wand for 2 seconds. Record as many (or as few) swishes per person as you like — that's the whole point.
