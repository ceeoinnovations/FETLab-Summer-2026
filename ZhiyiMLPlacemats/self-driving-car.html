Car setup

45

Calibrate once: prop the car up so the wheels spin free, connect over Bluetooth, then press each test button — the wheel should try to roll the car forward. If it spins backward, tick that side's “Reverse” box and test again. No Bluetooth? Use the Python bridge instead.

Moves to learn

Hold a capture button while showing the camera that move — grab 30–60 examples each, with small variations. The action menu decides what the car actually does when this move wins. You need examples in at least two moves before predicting.

Runs entirely in your browser with TensorFlow.js — nothing is uploaded. The car connects directly over Bluetooth in Chrome or Edge, or through the optional Python bridge.
